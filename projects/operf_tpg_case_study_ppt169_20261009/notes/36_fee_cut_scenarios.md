The fee cut helps in every tested growth case, but the size of the improvement depends on the measure. OPERF's NPV gain rises with growth, from $3.55 million at 0% to $9.99 million at 30%, while the IRR gain falls from about 31 basis points to about 20. At 10% growth the IRR improves from 6.42% to 6.65% but stays well below the 15% benchmark. These are single-variable scenarios with other assumptions fixed, not probabilities, and the model does not test whether a lower fee changes how the GP behaves, so this is not evidence that the fee cut has no effect on incentives.
