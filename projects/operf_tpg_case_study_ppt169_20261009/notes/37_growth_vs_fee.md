Start from the same baseline of 20% growth and a 1.50% fee. Lowering growth by one percentage point to 19% takes OPERF's NPV from plus $21.68 million to minus $6.41 million, a loss of $28.09 million. Lowering only the fee to 1.35% adds $6.55 million. The loss is about 4.3 times the benefit, so in this specific comparison the fee cut provides little cushion against weaker performance. One point means one percentage point, the two bars are not additive, and the 4.3 ratio holds only for this comparison. The end-of-life exit assumption also still matters: the concentration of cash in Year 10 is not resolved by the fee cut.

Detail. Break-even growth is about 19.23% at the 1.50% fee and about 19.00% at 1.35%, both interpolated, so 19% is approximately rather than exactly break-even.
