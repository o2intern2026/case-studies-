Hold the growth and exit schedule fixed. With no fees and no carry, LP IRR is 20%. Adding management fees takes it to 17.80%, and adding carry takes it to 15.72%. In that order, the two steps are about 220 and 208 basis points, roughly 428 basis points in total. These differences come from recomputing the full cash flows, not from subtracting the 1.50% fee rate or the 20% carry rate, and the split depends on the order in which the charges are added.
