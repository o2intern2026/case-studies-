Now return to the WaMu stake that appeared in the structure diagram. On 11 April 2008, Washington Mutual, Inc. disclosed an investment agreement that named TPG VI. On 25 September 2008, Washington Mutual Bank was closed. Note that the holding company and the bank are different legal entities, and the timeline is a sequence, not to scale. By December, limited partners had reason to scrutinise TPG's investment selection and risk assessment. That may add to TPG's pressure to maintain investor confidence, but one investment does not represent the performance of the whole fund. Beyond how they view the manager, LPs faced a more practical problem: when distributions are uncertain, what happens to their existing commitments and their asset allocation?
