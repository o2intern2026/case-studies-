Question 3 in summary: the two sides' cash flows carry different risks. The LP receives investment distributions, and its IRR and NPV move with growth and timing, with capital committed before any return is realised. The GP receives management fees and carry; carry changes with fund performance, and the GP also has about $500 million of its own capital at stake and operating costs to cover. Fixed fees support part of the GP's income, but they do not eliminate GP risk, so the receipts chart is not a picture of net profit or of the full risk position. That gives Question 4 a precise job: how much does lowering the fixed fee improve things?
