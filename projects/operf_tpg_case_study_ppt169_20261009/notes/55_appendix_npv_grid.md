Reference slide. The grid shows the NPV of the marginal $75 million inception commitment, in millions, across asset growth rates in the rows and assumed discount rates in the columns; the columns are assumptions, not independently established OPERF required returns. Positive values favour retaining within the model and negative values favour the hypothetical lower inception commitment. The base case, 20% growth at 15% discounting, is plus $2.8 million. The extra tranche is worth keeping only when growth is high relative to the discount rate: at 15% discounting it turns negative at 18% growth, and at 20% discounting it is negative until growth reaches 25%. At 10% discounting it is positive from 15% growth upwards. Source is Q6 OPERF decision cells A39 to F48. These are inception-date values; explain that limitation if anyone applies the grid directly to the December 2008 cancellation.
