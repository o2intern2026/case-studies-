The model's endpoints are a hurdle high enough to eliminate carry, or zero carry. Those are not automatically reasonable requests, so we distinguish the LP-value optimum within a chosen range from a reasonable negotiated term, which also needs market and contract evidence. Now evaluate the terms Question 5 actually asks about, scaled to the $750 million commitment with the workbook timing and the 1.35% fee. At the case inputs, the 8% hurdle adds $2.73 million with full catch-up and $20.65 million without it. Cutting carry from 20% to 15% adds $17.67 million in the isolated no-hurdle comparison.
