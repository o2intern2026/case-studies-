Start with the external shock to this investment structure. The crisis hit through two channels. In the financing channel, the credit contraction made it harder to finance acquisitions and refinance debt, which limits what a fund can buy and support. In the exit channel, falling public-market valuations and an uncertain outlook weakened sale valuations, IPO prospects and exit timing, so getting capital back became slower and less certain. These are industry-level shocks. The transmission mechanism here is the team's analysis, read with the Federal Reserve's statement of 16 December 2008 on credit conditions and the weaker outlook.
