With that map, the letter's effects are clear. The optional 10% reduction would take OPERF's commitment from $750 million to $675 million. The fee reduction, offered to all LPs, takes the management fee from 1.50% to 1.35%. And calls above 30% of commitments in the following year would need advisory-committee approval, which is a conditional limit on capital calls, not an absolute cap. OPERF has to decide whether to accept the reduction and whether to seek other terms. Why would TPG make these concessions at this moment?
