The two parties are a pension investor and a private-equity manager. OPERF is Oregon's public pension investor and a limited partner in this case. TPG is the private-equity firm that manages TPG Partners VI as its general partner. OPERF committed $750 million in February 2008, which is about 3.8% of the fund's $19.8 billion of commitments. So this is a question about adjusting an existing commitment, not about making a new one. From here we look only at the fund that carries that commitment. The footnote matters: the 3.8% is OPERF's share of the fund's committed capital, not its asset allocation and not paid-in capital.
