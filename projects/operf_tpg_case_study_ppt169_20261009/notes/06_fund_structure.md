Read the diagram top to bottom first. TPG, meaning its management and GP entities, selects and manages investments, and TPG VI, the fund, holds them, directly or through vehicles. Then read the side flows. OPERF and the other limited partners pay in capital when it is called, and TPG also puts in its own capital. When an investment is realised, the proceeds come back to the fund first and are then distributed under the partnership agreement. The GP's return on its own capital is shown separately from its fees and conditional carry. The point is that the results of the underlying investments, including the WaMu equity stake you can see here, reach the investors only through the fund. Keep this map in mind, because the December letter changes the commitment, the fees and the call arrangements that sit on these very flows.
