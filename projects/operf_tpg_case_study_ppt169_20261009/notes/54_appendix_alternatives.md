Every modelled compensation alternative is compared with the fee-cut baseline of a 1.35% fee, 20% carry and no hurdle, at 20% growth and a 15% discount rate, with NPV at model inception and nominal carry and GP present value for the whole fund. The baseline gives a 15.94% LP IRR, $7.16 billion of nominal GP carry and $3.44 billion of GP compensation present value. A full-catch-up hurdle adds only 9 basis points and $2.73 million. A no-catch-up hurdle adds 66 basis points and $20.65 million. Fifteen percent carry with no hurdle adds 55 basis points and $17.67 million. The full-catch-up hurdle with 15% carry adds 61 basis points and $19.31 million. The no-catch-up hurdle with 15% carry is the model's maximum at 103 basis points and $33.15 million, with GP compensation present value falling to $2.56 billion. The gains share the same no-hurdle baseline, so if the full-catch-up hurdle were already in place, adding 15% carry would give about $16.59 million, which is $19.31 million minus $2.73 million unrounded. The approximate fee-equivalent column uses $11.65 million of whole-fund LP NPV per basis point of management fee from Q5 Analysis cell C18; it is a base-case comparison, not proof of equal incentives or available fee offsets. Sources are Q5 Analysis cells B6 to G18 and Engine rows 12 to 13.
