Each concession has an economic cost, but each can also make funding more predictable and strengthen the relationship. The commitment option can ease funding strain and reduce the risk of LPs failing to meet calls, at the cost of less deployable capital if LPs exercise it. The fee cut shares the burden and supports LP confidence, at the cost of lower fee revenue. The call-approval threshold improves cash-planning predictability, at the cost of an extra approval step above the threshold. Our conclusion for Question 1 is that TPG could accept limited economic concessions to preserve reliable LP support while keeping the fund able to invest. This is a possible explanation; it does not prove that defaults were avoided or that confidence was restored.
